Core Features

🔒 100% Offline & Private

No account setup, no ads, and no internet required. Every record is stored safely on your device.

📊 Smart Allowances

Set monthly targets and savings reserves. Pocket Expense dynamically calculates your daily spending allowance.

📱 Interactive Widgets

Detailed and Compact Android widgets for your home screen with quick expense shortcuts and privacy mode.

📂 Full Data Ownership

Export your records to CSV, print beautiful monthly A4 PDF reports, and create offline JSON backups anytime.

⌁ Recurring Bill Reminders

Never miss a monthly bill. Review due reminders and pay with one tap without automatic charges.

📈 Weekly Insights

Track spending trends across 8 categories (Food, Groceries, Transport, Bills, Shopping, Health, Family, Other).
