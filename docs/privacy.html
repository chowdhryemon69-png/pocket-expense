Privacy Policy for Pocket Expense

Effective Date: October 2026 · Developer: Chowdhury Emon

Your privacy is fundamental to Pocket Expense. Pocket Expense is built from the ground up as a 100% offline, local-first application. We do not collect, track, store, transmit, or share any personal information or financial data.

1. Local Storage

All expense entries, budgets, recurring bill reminders, and settings are stored strictly on your device in Android local application storage (via Android SharedPreferences). Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly create a manual export or backup file.

2. Network & Third-Party Access

Pocket Expense operates entirely offline:

  • No Internet Permission: The app does not request or use Android network permissions (android.permission.INTERNET).
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not integrate tracking, crash reporting, telemetry, or analytics SDKs.
  • No Advertising: Pocket Expense contains zero advertisements or ad networks.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide email/phone numbers to use the app.

3. System Permissions & File Pickers

Pocket Expense uses system File Pickers (ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT and ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT) only when you initiate an action:

  • Exporting Data: Saving JSON backups or CSV expense sheets to a storage location you select.
  • Restoring Backups: Reading a JSON backup file that you select from your device storage.

4. Android Widgets & Home Screen Privacy

Pocket Expense includes optional Home Screen Widgets (Detailed and Compact). Widgets read your local device storage to show spending totals and provide quick shortcuts. A Home-screen privacy feature is available inside the app settings to hide monetary amounts on widgets, replacing numbers with ••••.

5. PDF Reports & Printing

Monthly PDF report generation and printing are handled on-device using Android System Print Services (PrintManager). No external web servers or cloud converters are involved.

6. Data Control & Deletion

You have total ownership and control over your financial records:

  • Expense Removal: Deleting an entry inside the app immediately removes it from local storage.
  • Full Data Wipe: Uninstalling Pocket Expense completely removes all application storage and local database records from your device. You can create a JSON backup before uninstalling if you wish to preserve your data.
